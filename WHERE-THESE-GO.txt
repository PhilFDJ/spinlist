THE ALERT
---------
Red, full width, under the header, flashing — in a dark room at 11pm it has
to be impossible to miss. Shows what was heard, which list it's on, and the
couple's own note if they left one, which is usually the most persuasive
thing on screen. Stays until you tap "Got it".

A weak match shows amber instead: "Was that this one?" with the heard title
against the planned one, Yes/No. Yes ticks it, No dismisses. A clean match
shows a brief green "Ticked off" that clears itself after six seconds.
An unrecognised track shows nothing at all.

BANNED BLOCKS IN THE MUSIC TAB
------------------------------
Shown with ⛔, red heading, tinted background, and NO play ticks — ticking
"played" against a forbidden song is meaningless, and a tappable tick
invites the mistake. Confirmed by test: zero clickable elements in a
banned block.

WHY POLLING AND NOT A SOCKET
----------------------------
Venue wifi drops constantly, and a dead socket fails silently — you'd never
know the alarm had stopped working. A failed poll just tries again in four
seconds. The full wedding refresh stays at 30s; only the alert is quick.

CHECKED IN A REAL BROWSER
-------------------------
  - banned track  -> red, flashing, couple's note shown, dismiss works,
                     and it stays dismissed rather than re-firing
  - weak match    -> amber, Yes ticks the right song, No dismisses
  - clean match   -> green, fades by itself after 6s
  - filler track  -> silent
  - reduced motion-> red but not strobing
  - 320px wide    -> no overflow, buttons reachable

STILL ON YOUR SIDE
------------------
Music Manager has to read Serato and post the track. Remember the dwell
timer — Serato's history records loads and ejects, not play time, so
without a delay every quick audition ticks a song as played. The BAN alarm
should fire immediately on load; only the auto-tick should wait.
